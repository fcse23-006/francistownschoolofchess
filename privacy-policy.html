Privacy Policy

How Francistown School of Chess collects, stores, and protects student and parent information.

Back to Home
Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Our Commitment: At Francistown School of Chess, we respect and safeguard the privacy of all students, parents, and visitors. We adhere to responsible data protection standards and will never sell personal information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality chess coaching, register players for FIDE-rated events, and communicate tournament logistics, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Parent or guardian name, student name, email address, telephone and WhatsApp contact numbers.
  • Student Profile: Age, date of birth (required for FIDE age category verification), and current chess rating or tournament history.
  • Instructional Records: Attendance records, homework assignments, tournament game notation, and internal academy skill evaluations.
  • Payment Records: Transaction reference receipts and billing status (we do not store sensitive credit card numbers directly on our servers).

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely for legitimate instructional and operational purposes:

  • Organizing small group cohorts matched by age and tactical skill level.
  • Submitting verified tournament rosters to regional and national chess federations.
  • Sending schedule updates, holiday clinic notices, and student progress reports.
  • Addressing parent inquiries regarding coaching plans and billing invoices.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement standard organizational and digital measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of student records. Access to private contact rosters is strictly restricted to authorized academy administrative staff and certified coaches.

4. Third-Party Platforms

For online training, our students may utilize third-party tools such as Zoom and online chess platforms (e.g. Lichess.org). We recommend parents review the independent privacy policies of these platforms.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Inquiries

Parents and students may request to review, update, or delete personal contact records at any time by contacting our academy administration at info@francistownschoolofchess.com.
