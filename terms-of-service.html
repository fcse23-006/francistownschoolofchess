Terms of Service

Guidelines, academy etiquette, and enrollment conditions at Francistown School of Chess.

Back to Home
Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Welcome: By enrolling in classes, participating in tournaments, or using the website of Francistown School of Chess, you agree to comply with the following academy rules and conditions.

1. Academy Etiquette & Code of Conduct

Francistown School of Chess emphasizes classical sportsmanship and respect:

  • Students must shake hands with opponents before and after every game.
  • FIDE touch-move rules apply during all structured studio games and tournament matches.
  • Distracting behavior, disrespectful speech, or disruptive phone use during lecture and tournament sessions is strictly prohibited.
  • We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against bullying or harassment.

2. Enrollment & Tuition Fees

Tuition fees support certified coaches, tournament boards, digital clocks, and instructional materials:

  • Monthly tuition (e.g. BWP 300 for standard programs) is due on or before the 1st day of each calendar month.
  • Fees ensure reserved seating in small group cohorts (max 1:4 to 1:6 coach ratio).
  • Make-up sessions for verified absences may be scheduled in parallel online cohorts subject to seat availability.

3. Equipment Care & Liability

Tournament Staunton chess sets, DGT digital clocks, and demonstration boards provided in the studio are precision instructional equipment. Students are taught to treat all equipment with care and return pieces to designated cases after play.

4. Media & Photography Permissions

Photographs and video clips taken during tournaments and academy award ceremonies may occasionally be published on our official website or newsletter. Parents who prefer their child not be photographed may opt out by notifying academy staff in writing.

5. Amendments to Terms

Francistown School of Chess reserves the right to update these terms to reflect evolving instructional standards and tournament schedules. Current terms will remain posted on our website.
