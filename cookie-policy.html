Cookie Policy

Information on how cookies and browser storage are utilized on our website.

Back to Home
Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Overview: This Cookie Policy outlines how Francistown School of Chess uses cookies and similar technologies to improve browsing reliability, remember preferences, and analyze website traffic.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when visiting websites. They help websites remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, ensuring a seamless user experience.

2. Cookies We Utilize

Category Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Necessary for site navigation, form security, and responsive layouts. Session
Performance & Analytics Anonymous metrics regarding visitor count and popular program pages to help improve site content. Up to 12 months
Functional Cookies Remembers user preferences such as dismissed banners and preferred class formats. Persistent

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive features on our site.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our use of cookies or privacy practices, reach us via email at info@francistownschoolofchess.com.
