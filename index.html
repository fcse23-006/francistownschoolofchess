The Standard in Chess Education

We blend analytical discipline, competitive tournament preparation, and engaging pedagogy to unlock every student's cognitive potential.

Elite FIDE Coaching

Learn directly from Botswana National Team coaches and certified masters with deep pedagogical experience in youth development.

Tournament Readiness

Weekly clock training, Swiss-system club tourneys, and comprehensive preparation for national and regional championships.

Systematic Progression

Regular tactical evaluations, game notation reviews, personalized opening repertoires, and individual rating goal tracking.

Hybrid Learning

Choose between our vibrant Francistown physical academy, live interactive Zoom classrooms, or flexible private sessions.

Small Coach-to-Student Ratio

Classes capped at a strict 1:4 or 1:6 ratio to ensure individualized feedback, personalized problem solving, and attention.

Life Skills & Focus

Chess sharpens patience, risk assessment, spatial awareness, and resilience—transferable qualities in academics and life.

Building Character Beyond the Board

At Francistown School of Chess, intellectual rigour walks hand in hand with sportsmanship. We foster an encouraging environment where students learn how to win with grace and analyze defeats with curiosity.

  • Respect, mutual handshake culture, and classical tournament etiquette.
  • Constructive post-game analysis without fear of making mistakes.
  • Lifelong friendships within Botswana's vibrant chess community.
