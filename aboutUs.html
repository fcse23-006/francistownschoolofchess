About Francistown School of Chess

A bonafide affiliate member of Botswana Chess Federation, dedicated to developing grassroots youth chess, providing world-class coaching, and shaping leaders on and off the board.

Developing Chess at Grassroots Level

Francistown School of Chess is based in Francistown and is a bonafide affiliate member of the Botswana Chess Federation.

The primary aim of the academy is to develop chess at the grassroots level, reaching out to the youth, giving them top quality training, and exposing them to high-profile competitive tournaments.

To achieve this, the academy actively organizes and runs youth tournaments, instructional workshops for young children, and certified training programs for upcoming chess coaches across Botswana.

Over the years, Francistown School of Chess has grown in leaps and bounds to become a reputable brand both locally and internationally. Any association with our institution stands to take any brand and young mind to another level.

International Standing & Global Visitors

Because of our reputation in the global chess world, world-renowned leaders have visited and partnered with our academy.

Ms. Beatriz Marinello

Former International Chess Federation (FIDE) Vice President & Former US Chess Federation President

In March 2018, Francistown School of Chess was privileged to host Ms. Beatriz Marinello during her historic visit to Botswana, recognizing our grassroots youth initiatives and coaching excellence.

Mr. Robert Katende

Inspiration behind the Disney Movie "Queen of Katwe" & FIDE Social Commission

The Academy was honored to host Mr. Robert Katende, whose pioneering work in mentoring young underprivileged chess prodigies inspired the world. His visit reinforced our commitment to transformative community coaching.

Social Responsibility & Community Impact

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to experience the strategic beauty of chess.

Adoption of Underprivileged Youth

The Academy prides itself on the formal adoption and sponsorship of underprivileged and disadvantaged children from our community, providing free full-time coaching, tournament entry fees, and mentorship.

60 Chess Sets Donated

The Academy has donated 60 complete tournament chess sets to Primary Schools across the North East District, enabling children in remote areas to play and learn the game.

Specialized Community Support

Dedicated chess sets, instructional workshops, and demonstration sessions have been donated directly to the Francistown Centre for the Deaf and the Francistown SOS Children's Village.
